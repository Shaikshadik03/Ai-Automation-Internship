# What is Automation?

## What is automation?
Automation means letting tools do boring, repetitive work for us. Tools like n8n and Make connect apps together, and APIs let them share data. AI can be added in the middle so the workflow can think and decide. The result: we save time and can spend it on studying or more important work.

## Trigger, Process and Action
- **Trigger:** the event that starts the workflow. For example, a new email arrives, a form is submitted, or a schedule hits a set time.
- **Process:** what happens to the data in the middle. It is cleaned, checked and analysed, and this is where AI decides what to do.
- **Action:** the final result in the real world, like sending a message, saving a row, or making a call.

## Example 1: Daily life (Gmail filter)
- **Trigger:** a new email arrives
- **Process:** AI reads it and decides if it is spam, promotional or important
- **Action:** important mails go to Telegram with a short summary, and the rest are labelled and hidden

Result: I never miss an important mail and don't waste time reading all of them.

## Example 2: Business (AI voice agent)
- **Trigger:** a batch of phone numbers is uploaded
- **Process:** the AI calls each number, explains the business, and understands whether the customer is interested
- **Action:** if interested, the name, number and needs are saved in a spreadsheet

Result: the business owner only calls back interested customers, so no one is annoyed and more clients are won.
